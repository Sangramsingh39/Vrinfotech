Boosting Local Traffic: The Web Storefront Blueprint

Written by Pune Local Tech Team | 6 Min Read

For small local businesses from FC Road to Kothrud, having a digital presence is no longer optional. A responsive, mobile-first website acts as your virtual storefront. Cafe and restaurant owners can display interactive digital menus that load instantly on mobile networks, preventing visitor bounce.

Photographers can showcase high-definition galleries using lazy-loading tech to display portfolios without slowing down page load. For antique and art sellers, a simple product gallery with WhatsApp integration lets local customers browse pieces and initiate purchases directly.

AI & Automation

Voice Calling Agents: The Future of Outbound Sales

Written by AI Research Lab | 8 Min Read

Outbound lead generation has historically been a labor-intensive operation. Standard dialers require humans to spend hours placing calls, handling repetitive questions, and manually entering results. AI voice calling agents represent a major shift.

Built using natural language understanding (NLU) and low-latency speech synthesis, these bots speak to prospects in real-time. They can qualify leads (e.g. asking a home buyer their budget and location preferences), schedule site visits, and advertise seasonal discounts.

Because they process calls concurrently, they can dial thousands of prospects in minutes, pre-filtering interested leads and sending qualified details straight to your sales team's CRM.

Enterprise Systems

From Excel to LLM Pipelines: Invoice Processing

Written by Systems Engineering Dept | 7 Min Read

Manufacturing and logistics firms in industrial zones like Chakan and Bhosari MIDC process hundreds of vendor invoices weekly. In many offices, this data entry is still manual: staff read emails, open PDFs, and manually copy invoice numbers, dates, and amounts into Excel files. This process is slow and prone to human error.

By deploying an AI-driven invoice extraction pipeline, we automate the complete cycle. An AI model scans incoming emails, extracts invoice files, runs optical character recognition (OCR) to parse fields, classifies the line items, and logs them directly into a database or ERP system.

Staff only need to review flagged anomalies, reducing administrative overhead by up to 80% and ensuring perfect logging accuracy.
